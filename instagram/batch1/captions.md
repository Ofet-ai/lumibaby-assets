## Carousel: 02 → 03 → 04 — I1 Baby Poop Color Guide
**Caption:**
Save this before the next diaper surprises you. 💛
Most colors are normal. A few mean you should call your pediatrician. Swipe for the full guide:

🟡 Yellow / mustard: normal for breastfed babies
🟤 Brown: normal for formula-fed babies and babies on solids
🟢 Green: usually normal
⚫ Black: normal in the first 2–3 days (meconium). After the first week, call your pediatrician.
🔴 Red: may mean blood. Call your pediatrician.
⚪ White or very pale: call your pediatrician right away or go to the ER.

When you log a diaper in LumiBaby, the app explains the color you picked.

General info, not medical advice. If something seems off, trust your gut and ask your pediatrician.

**Hashtags:** #LumiBaby #babypoop #newborntips #newmomtips #babycare
